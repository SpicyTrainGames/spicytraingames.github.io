About the Game

PactSworn is a bullet heaven roguelike game where you play as a warlock to cut through endless hordes of eldritch monsters.

Fight through various randomly generated maps - each with their own different challenges - in order to find and purge the source of the corruption! Level up and equip weapons, traits, and augments to design potent builds and see how far you can make it!

◆
Weapons and Traits

Collect various weapons with different kinds of attacks to assemble your build! Each weapon has associated traits, which grant additional effects to your character to provide even more power!

◆
Characters and Abilities

Play as various different characters with different abilities and play styles to find the perfect build that works for you!

◆
Warlocks & Eldritch Monsters

Try out a variety of warlock characters to take down different eldritch monsters!

◆
Levels & Maps

Make your way throughout various maps, defeating different bosses along the way to discover the secrets that lie ahead!
